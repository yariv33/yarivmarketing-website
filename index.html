How It Works

Up and running
in 3 steps.

1

Download the App

Get Creator Stats Widget from the App Store. It's a native iOS app built for iPhone and iPad.

2

Log in securely to TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, and X using each platform's official login flow.

3

Add to Home Screen

Long-press your home screen, tap +, find Creator Stats Widget, choose a size, and you're done.
