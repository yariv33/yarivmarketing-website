Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026  ·  Effective date: March 2026

We built Creator Stats Widget with privacy in mind. We do not sell your data. We do not share your personal information with third parties for advertising purposes. We only access what is needed to show you your stats.

1. Who We Are

Creator Stats Widget ("we", "us", "our") is an iOS application developed and operated by Yariv Marketing, reachable at yariv33@gmail.com. Our website is yarivmarketing.com.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our app.

2. Information We Collect

Account Connection Data
When you connect a social media account (TikTok, Instagram, YouTube, or X), we use that platform's official OAuth authentication. We receive an access token that allows us to fetch your public stats on your behalf. We do not receive or store your username or password.

Stats Data
We fetch publicly available statistics from your connected accounts, such as follower count, view count, and engagement metrics. This data is displayed in the app and widget. It is stored temporarily on your device and refreshed automatically.

Device Information
We may collect basic device information such as your iOS version and device type to provide technical support and improve the app.

In-App Purchases
If you make an in-app purchase, the transaction is processed entirely by Apple via the App Store. We do not receive or store your payment information.

Support Communications
If you contact us by email, we will retain your message to respond to your inquiry.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To display your social media statistics in the app and home screen widget
  • To authenticate your connected accounts via official platform OAuth flows
  • To refresh your stats periodically so they stay up to date
  • To respond to your support requests
  • To improve the app's performance and fix bugs

4. Third-Party Platforms

Creator Stats Widget integrates with the following third-party platforms using their official APIs. By connecting your account, you agree to their respective terms and privacy policies:

  • TikTok – Privacy Policy
  • Instagram / Meta – Privacy Policy
  • YouTube / Google – Privacy Policy
  • X (Twitter) – Privacy Policy

We only request the minimum permissions necessary to display your stats. We do not post on your behalf, access your messages, or modify your accounts in any way.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third parties for marketing or advertising purposes. We do not share your information with anyone except:

  • When required by law, court order, or governmental authority
  • To protect the safety, rights, or property of our users or the public

6. Data Storage & Security

Your access tokens and stats data are stored locally on your device using Apple's secure storage mechanisms. We use industry-standard security practices to protect any data we handle. No system is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

7. Data Retention

Stats data is cached on your device and refreshed automatically. You can disconnect any platform account at any time from within the app, which will delete the associated access token from your device. If you delete the app, all locally stored data is removed.

8. Children's Privacy

Creator Stats Widget is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

9. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent to data processing
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at yariv33@gmail.com.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
